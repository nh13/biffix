# About Settings

Your own choices: whether biffix shows the Lab or the Expert view, light or dark, your pinned pipelines, and who to ask when an analysis needs attention. The bell at the top right tells you when your analyses finish; email notifications aren't available yet.

## What's the difference between Lab and Expert?

Lab shows analyses, samples and results in plain words. Expert shows the jobs, logs, costs and AWS details behind them. Switch at any time with **Lab | Expert** at the top right of every page.

## Can I see the tour again?

Yes: **Replay the tour** takes you back to My work and shows it. If you dismissed the "Switch to Expert" hint, you can bring it back here too.

## Where are the site settings?

The site's title and logo, reference genomes, engines and alerts are in the Expert view.
