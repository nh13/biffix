# About Data

The data folders connected to biffix, such as your sequencing core's deliveries, and your project's own storage, where results land.

## How do I find my FASTQ files?

Open the folder your core delivered to and go down to your run. Search finds a file by name under the folder you are in.

## Can I look inside a file?

Tables, text, images, PDFs and HTML reports open in the viewer here.

## Why can't I see a folder?

An admin connects data folders in the deploy config. Ask them if one you need is missing.
