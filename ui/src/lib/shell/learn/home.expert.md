# About Home

Home counts every run in your projects by state, with links into the filtered runs list, the service status and the registered workflows.

## How do I launch a run?

Click **New run**, pick a workflow version and fill in its form. The form is built from the workflow's own parameter schema.

## Where do I see what's running?

The **Active** card opens the runs list filtered to live runs. Each run's page has its jobs, logs, graph and cost.

## Is the service healthy?

The Status item in the sidebar is tinted by overall health. Its page shows each part: the API, the database, event ingest, the background sync, AWS Batch and the alarms.
