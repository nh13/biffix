# About My work

My work is your start page: the analyses you started that need a look, the ones still running, the ones that just finished, the experiments they are in, and the pipelines you pinned.

## How do I start an analysis?

Click **Start an analysis** and pick a pipeline. Then **Add samples → Choose files…** to pick your sequencing files, and check the reference and options. Nothing runs until you click **Launch analysis**.

## What does "Needs attention" mean?

Your analyses that failed and that you haven't dismissed. Opening one does not clear it: its page names the sample and step that went wrong, and it stays here until you click **Dismiss** on it (or **Dismiss these** for the ones listed), or **Try again** on it. The bell is separate: it counts your unread notifications and how many of those stopped with a problem, and **Mark all read** only marks them read.

## How do I keep a pipeline handy?

Star it on the Pipelines page. Pinned pipelines show at the bottom of My work, one click from **Start**.
