# About experiments

An experiment is a named group of analyses, such as one study. Its page shows their results side by side and what they cost in total.

## How do I make one?

Click **New experiment** and give it a name. Then start an analysis into it, or add one you already ran from its page.

## Can an analysis be in two experiments?

No, one at a time. On its page, **Experiment ▾** then **Move** puts it in another; nothing is copied or deleted.

## Who can see it?

Everyone with access to its project.
