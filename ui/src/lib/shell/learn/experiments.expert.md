# About Experiments

Named groups of runs within a project, for reading their results together. An experiment holds references to runs, not copies.

## How do I create one?

**New experiment** here. Add a run from its page with **Add to experiment**; a run is in at most one.

## What does the page show?

Its runs and their states, their reports side by side, a sample × run table, and the summed cost.

## Is it scoped to a project?

Yes. An experiment and its runs belong to one project, and that project's access applies.
