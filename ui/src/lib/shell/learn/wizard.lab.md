# About starting an analysis

Four short steps: pick a pipeline, add your samples, choose the reference genome and options, then review and launch. You can go back at any point; nothing runs until you click **Launch analysis**.

## How do I add my samples?

**Add samples → Choose files…**, then open the folder your sequencing files are in and tick them. biffix pairs the reads by sample and builds the samplesheet; check the sample names before you go on.

## Which reference should I choose?

The genome your samples come from, such as GRCh38 for human or GRCm39 for mouse. An admin registers the ones on offer.

## What will it cost?

The review step shows an estimate from this pipeline's past analyses, once it has enough of them.
