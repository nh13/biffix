# About Analyses

Your analyses, or everyone's in your projects, newest first. Filter to the ones in progress, finished, needing attention or failed, or search by name.

## How do I find one analysis?

Type part of its name or pipeline in the search box, or pick a filter. **Everyone's** shows the analyses others in your projects started.

## What do the status words mean?

_Getting ready_ and _Waiting to start_ come before any sample runs. _Running_ counts the samples done. _Needs attention_ means it failed. The **Needs attention** filter lists only your failed analyses you haven't dismissed (**Dismiss** on a row, or **Dismiss these** / **Dismiss all** for the rows on the page); **Failed** lists them all.

## Can I compare analyses?

Put them in one experiment: open an analysis and choose **Add to experiment**. The experiment's page shows their results side by side.
